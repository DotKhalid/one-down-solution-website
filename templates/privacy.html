What this website collects

When you submit an enquiry, we store your name, email, optional phone number, selected service, budget range, message and submission time. We use these details to respond to your project enquiry and manage follow-up.

How it is stored

Enquiries are stored in the website’s database and are visible through a password-protected administrator inbox. This website does not send automated marketing emails or sell contact form submissions.

Cookies and security

A session cookie is used for form security and administrator sign-in. This build does not include advertising or analytics trackers. For abuse prevention, the connecting IP address and request time are temporarily recorded; entries older than one hour are removed when the next rate-limited request is processed. Hosting providers may maintain their own access logs.
